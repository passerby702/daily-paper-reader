## 摘要
准确的免疫受体设计需要对抗体、纳米抗体和T细胞受体中氨基酸序列、全原子构象以及靶标结合几何的耦合变化进行建模。现有方法通常只解决部分问题，例如将结构生成与序列设计分离、依赖固定主链反向折叠，或者仅关注单一受体类别。我们提出了IgGM2，一个统一的用于免疫受体结构预测和CDR序列-结构协同设计的全原子生成框架。IgGM2采用从结构到设计的策略：首先学习免疫受体如何围绕固定靶标结构定位，然后将这种以靶标为条件的结构先验迁移到CDR设计中。与模块化设计流程不同，IgGM2联合生成CDR残基身份和全原子受体结构，使框架结构能够适应设计的CDR，无需单独的反向折叠或外部侧链堆积。与基于虚拟原子几何的连续残基编码不同，IgGM2保持序列预测的明确性，同时仅使用原子14占位符进行全原子表示。在结构预测基准测试中，IgGM2在FoldBench上比AlphaFold3更好地捕捉了受体-靶标空间关系，并在TCR-pMHC建模上取得了强劲性能。在序列设计基准测试中，IgGM2实现了有竞争力的氨基酸恢复率，并改进了基于Rosetta的界面偏好指标，表明其生成的结合界面更有利。这些结果支持IgGM2作为适应性免疫受体结构预测和设计的统一全原子框架。

## Abstract
Accurate immune receptor design requires modeling the coupled variation of aminoacid sequence, full-atom conformation, and target-binding geometry across antibodies, nanobodies, and T-cell receptors (TCRs). Existing methods often address only part of this problem, either by separating structure generation from sequence design, relying on fixed-backbone inverse folding, or focusing on a single receptor class. We introduce IgGM2, a unified all-atom generative framework for immune receptor structure prediction and CDR sequence-structure co-design. IgGM2 follows a structure-to-design strategy: it first learns how immune receptors are positioned around fixed target structures, and then transfers this target-conditioned structural prior to CDR design. Unlike modular design pipelines, IgGM2 jointly generates CDR residue identities and full-atom receptor structures, allowing frame-work geometry to adapt to designed CDRs without separate inverse folding or external sidechain packing. Unlike continuous residue encodings based on virtualatom geometry, IgGM2 keeps sequence prediction explicit while using atom14 placeholders only for full-atom representation. On structure prediction benchmarks, IgGM2 better captures receptor-target spatial relationships than AlphaFold3 on FoldBench and achieves strong performance on TCR-pMHC modeling. On sequence design benchmarks, IgGM2 achieves competitive amino-acid recovery and improves Rosetta-based interface preference metrics, suggesting more favorable generated binding interfaces. These results support IgGM2 as a unified all-atom framework for adaptive immune receptor structure prediction and design.
